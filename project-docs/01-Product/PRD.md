Cat Passport
Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Version: 1.0

Owner: Saurabh Sase

Platform: Mobile-first Responsive Website (Future PWA)

1. Vision

Cat Passport is an interactive community map that allows people to discover, revisit and document UAE's community cats.

Every cat has its own Passport.

The product transforms ordinary street cats into local celebrities with stories, photos and community memories.

The goal is not to build another map.

The goal is to encourage people to explore their neighbourhood and appreciate community cats.

2. Mission

Create the world's best platform for discovering, documenting and celebrating community cats.

4. Problem Statement

Community cats exist everywhere in the UAE.

People often:

Photograph them
Feed them
Visit them repeatedly

However:

There is no dedicated platform.
Google Maps mixes cat locations with every other type of place.
Cats have no identity or history.
Community knowledge is scattered across social media.

Cat Passport solves this problem.

5. Product Goal

Allow anyone to:

Discover nearby community cats.
View each cat's Passport.
Add sightings.
Upload photos.
Share stories.
Help build a living archive of UAE community cats.

16. Product Philosophy

Cat Passport is not a database.

It is not a GIS application.

It is not a social network.

It is a travel guide for discovering community cats.

Every cat should feel like someone worth visiting.

Every Passport should tell a story.

Every visit should create a memory.

17. Long-Term Vision

Cat Passport should become the default place people check before visiting an area in the UAE.

Instead of asking:

"Are there any cats nearby?"

People should instinctively open Cat Passport.

The platform should become a trusted, community-driven archive of the UAE's community cats while encouraging responsible interaction and appreciation of these animals.
